I think of Abraham and how he waited decades for a son. Something I recently found interesting in my studies is that the

In Mark 9:24, the man whose son was possessed and was seizing said:

"I do believe; help me overcome my unbelief!"


It’s a powerful lesson, because it shows that when we don’t see the light, it may be just a few more minutes until dawn.



I think science can actually be such an encouragement when it comes to faith. When we think of light, we


think of colors. Darkness is the absence of light—yet non-visible light exists even before dawn. The world is


bathed in invisible light from the sun before we ever see it.
